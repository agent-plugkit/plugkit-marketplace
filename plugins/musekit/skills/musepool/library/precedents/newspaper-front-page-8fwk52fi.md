# Typography

Stories two columns wide use a mid-size serif in capitals and lower case with an underline rule. Single-column stories use a condensed bold capital headline set in short lines. Beneath each headline sit one or two deck tiers in progressively smaller type, often centered and stepped narrower line by line, then body text in a small, justified serif.

The masthead is blackletter with an engraved vignette between the two halves of the title. Subheads inside stories are short bold lines set in the body column. Captions under the pictures are small capitals, centered, and are short, with a second line in smaller type.
